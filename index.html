Every Window Lit

Sit by a tower window and look after a small island town for a year, in twelve real weeks.

Each week follows real prices: fuel, power, food.

Seated, played with your hands (controllers work too). Made for ages 13 and up, gentle enough to play with family. Some weeks mention real natural disasters on the mainland; no damage or injuries are shown, and these weeks can be switched off.
