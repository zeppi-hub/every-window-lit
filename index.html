Every Window Lit

Sit by a tower window, open the shutters, and look after a small island city.

Each week follows real Italian prices: fuel, power, groceries.

Saved at week 1 of 12. Start over from week 1 Seated, played with your hands (controllers work too). Made for ages 13 and up, gentle enough to play with family. Some weeks mention real natural disasters on the mainland; no damage or injuries are shown, and these weeks can be switched off. Real data and credits
