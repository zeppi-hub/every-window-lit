## The measure

A period stops at ten deaths or more in one event, within 7 days of the period, on the country's land or coast, wrecks whose survivors or bodies came ashore there included. A wreck counts by the date it sank. The far part of a route is only noted. The month on the card is not a measure. Under ten, an event is weighed and listed, and an EMS record alone stops nothing. Every week here carries something; none is wholly clear.
