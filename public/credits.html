Every Window Lit · Data and credits

Season 1 replays real weekly and monthly price moves from public Italian data (Jan 2023 to Oct 2026). Ombrisa and everyone in it are fictional. World effects are scaled to each series' 2023–2026 range so they can be seen. Simplified game model, not policy advice.

Each in-game week shows one real period: a week for pump and wholesale power prices, a month for grocery prices. The game ships only the derived numbers: one percentage change per period, how it compares with the series' other changes, the next period's change (to settle the Sunday call) and a few changes before it.

Data

  • Pump prices. Fonte: Ministero dell'ambiente e della sicurezza energetica – Direzione generale fonti energetiche e titoli abilitativi, prezzi medi settimanali dei carburanti (sisen.mase.gov.it), accessed 31 Oct 2026, licensed CC BY 4.0. Changed: weekly percentage changes of the mean of Euro-super 95 and diesel prices, computed for the game.
  • Wholesale power prices. Ember, European Wholesale Electricity Price Data (ember-energy.org), accessed 31 Oct 2026, licensed CC BY 4.0; Ember's hourly source is the ENTSO-E Transparency Platform. Changed: weekly averages of Italy's daily day-ahead prices and their percentage changes, computed for the game.
  • Grocery prices. Fonte: Istat, Prezzi al consumo per l'intera collettività (NIC), "beni alimentari, per la cura della casa e della persona" (carrello della spesa), dataflow 167_745_DF_DCSP_NIC1B2025_6 (esploradati.istat.it), accessed 31 Oct 2026, licensed CC BY 4.0. Used as published: percentage change on the previous month, each shown as one in-game week; provisional months left out.
  • Weather. The data was obtained from the National Aeronautics and Space Administration (NASA) Langley Research Center (LaRC) Prediction Of Worldwide Energy Resources (POWER) Project funded through the NASA Earth Science Division (power.larc.nasa.gov). POWER Daily API v2.10.1, point near Naples, accessed 31 Oct 2026.

Weeks that overlap real disasters

Some weeks may overlap real natural disasters on the mainland. If they do, the game names the place and month in a letter, shows no damage or injuries, and lets the island respond only with help. These weeks can be switched off on the start page.
